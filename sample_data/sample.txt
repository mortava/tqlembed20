TQL Non-QM Lending Guidelines - Sample Document

DSCR Loan Program:
- Minimum DSCR: 1.00x (no-ratio allowed for certain scenarios)
- Loan amounts: $150,000 - $3,000,000
- LTV: Up to 80% purchase, 75% rate/term refi, 70% cash-out
- Credit score: 620 minimum
- Property types: SFR, 2-4 units, condos, townhomes

Bank Statement Program:
- 12 or 24 months personal or business bank statements
- Self-employed borrowers only
- Loan amounts: $150,000 - $3,000,000
- LTV: Up to 85% purchase
- Credit score: 640 minimum

Asset Depletion Program:
- Qualify using liquid assets divided over loan term
- No income documentation required
- Minimum assets: 110% of loan amount
- LTV: Up to 70%
- Credit score: 680 minimum

Cross Collateral Program:
- Use equity from existing properties as down payment
- No cash required from borrower
- Combined LTV: Up to 75%
- Minimum 2 properties required

Rate Lock Policy:
- 30-day locks available at par
- 45-day locks: +0.125 adjustment
- 60-day locks: +0.250 adjustment
- Extension available at 0.125/15 days
